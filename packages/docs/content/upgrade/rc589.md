**Concerns:** `pushword/core`

`image_inline.html.twig` is now a thin shim that delegates to the `image()` Twig function. If you have overridden `image_inline.html.twig` in your theme, your override still loads but the internal logic it relied on (link wrapping, wrapper element, `media_from_string` resolution) now lives in PHP (`MediaExtension::renderImage()`). Review your override and consider switching to the `image()` function directly. See [Template Changes](/upgrade/rc333#image-template-refactoring) for the full parameter reference.
