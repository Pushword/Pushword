# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Content editors and site administrators manage one or more multilingual websites from the Pushword admin. Developers also work with the same content through Git, Symfony extension points, and the API.

## Product Purpose

Pushword is a modular Symfony CMS for creating, managing, and maintaining websites from the admin, Git, or an AI agent. The interface should make routine content and extension workflows direct, legible, and dependable.

## Brand Personality

Neutral, clear, straightforward.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative product UI, unfamiliar replacements for standard controls, inconsistent component vocabulary, and visual emphasis that competes with the user's task.

## Design Principles

- Put the feature and its data first.
- Let familiar admin patterns carry the interaction.
- Make hierarchy clear before adding decoration.
- Deliver the simplest useful version and refine it against the real interface.
- Keep behavior and visual vocabulary consistent across screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve visible keyboard focus, pair color with text or icons, keep the DOM order logical, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
